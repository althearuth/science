Science Fair 2027 Registration Now Open

We're excited to announce that registration for the Annual School Science Fair 2027 is now officially open! This year's theme is "Sustainable Solutions: Science for Tomorrow." Students are encouraged to develop innovative projects that address environmental challenges and promote sustainability.
